Flowers photos

The main purpose of a flower is reproduction of the individual,[9] aiding in the survival of the species.[10] Flowers not only produce spores, which become gametophytes that produce sex cells, leading to fertilised cells, but also develop and help disseminate seeds.[11] Sexual reproduction between plants results in evolutionary adaptation, which improves species survival. Plants favour cross-pollination because it promotes the joining of sex cells from genetically distinct plants of the same species, thereby increasing genetic diversity. Facilitating this process is a key function of flowers and is often reflected in their form and structure.[11] Features designed to attract pollinators are among the most common adaptations.
